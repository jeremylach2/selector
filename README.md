# Selector

**[Watch a fly brain pick the next track →](https://selector-demo.vercel.app/watch)** A live run of the fruit fly's olfactory circuit, wired from the FlyWire connectome, choosing a song in your browser. Skip its pick and watch the synapses change.

**[Try it on your own Spotify export →](https://selector-demo.vercel.app)** Parsed in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.
